### QuickAI

**[QuickAI](https://github.com/everybodyai/quickai)** runs AI models on the computer you already have: chat, code,
vision, voice, documents, images and video. It is a small desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, written in Rust, and
open source under the MIT License.

- **Private by default.** The models run on your computer and the servers it starts listen on `127.0.0.1` only. It is
  designed to keep your prompts, the replies, your voice and your files there. No account, no telemetry.
- **Works out of the box.** One download comes with llama.cpp and the Bonsai 2 4B model, so the first chat needs no
  download at all. It runs on an ordinary office PC without a graphics card.
- **Three engines in one window.** llama.cpp built in; Ollama and ComfyUI installed when you ask.
- **For your other apps, too.** An OpenAI-compatible API on your own machine, MCP tools for your model, and
  `quickai-headless` for a server with no display.

### How we work

- **Your data stays yours.** Our tools process prompts and files on your device. Each product's privacy policy lists
  every host it can contact, and why.
- **Open source.** QuickAI's code is here, under the MIT License, for anyone to read, build, change and share.
- **Funded by help, not by data.** The tools are free. Organizations that roll them out can pay us for
  [deployment help and support](https://everybodyai.dev/organizations). No ads, and no sale of personal data.
