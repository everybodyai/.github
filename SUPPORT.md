# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [QuickAI's Discussions](https://github.com/everybodyai/quickai/discussions).
- **Something doesn't work:** open an issue in the repository it belongs to. For QuickAI, **Help → Report a problem**
  in the app fills one in for you, with your folders and names taken out.
- **How to use QuickAI:** press F1 in the app for Help with pictures of the window, or read
  [QuickAI's README](https://github.com/everybodyai/quickai#readme) and [its docs](https://github.com/everybodyai/quickai/tree/main/docs).
- **Security problems:** privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
- **Organizations** rolling out QuickAI can get paid help with deployment and support:
  [everybodyai.dev/organizations](https://everybodyai.dev/organizations).
- **Anything else:** [hello@everybodyai.dev](mailto:hello@everybodyai.dev).
